Learning records that can be replayed, inspected, and improved.

Helix Education is a component of Helix Codex: an event-sourced learning engine that keeps state on the machine, built around replayable events, sealed assessment, and inspectable progress. Built solo, self-taught, after a career switch.

Verified, snapshot 2026-08-29: 447 tests passing. Core event-sourced learning state implemented. External grounding and LLM services mocked or stubbed in tests. gRPC competency contracts exist; the service is not wired. No production client deployment. No external audit.

Why this exists

The alternatives either leak your learning material to a cloud or give you a chat window with no structure. Helix Education keeps the state on your machine: replayable, reconstructable, auditable. Progress is evidence, not guesswork.

Core capabilities

  • Event-sourced records with state reconstruction
  • Citation-grounded learning content
  • Sealed quiz scoring
  • Adaptive learning paths
  • Progress and milestone tracking
  • Portable, inspectable learning history
  • Zero AI dependency in the core engine

Honest boundary

The gRPC competency service is not wired. External grounding and LLM services are mocked or stubbed in tests. No production client deployment exists, and the engine is not yet integrated into Helix Prime. This is not a production deployment claim: there is no external audit, no certified data isolation, and no signed security review. No revenue has been realised.

The founder's story

I spent twenty-eight years in contact-centre operations and workforce management. Forecasting, scheduling, adherence, service levels, churn. The same problems appeared in every company I worked in, and none of the tools solved them properly.

In April 2026 I left that career and started building full time, alone, and teaching myself to write software as I went. The first four tools were published six weeks later, in May and June 2026. Each one took a single operational problem and solved it properly. They were not impressive. They were correct.

Those four tools converged into one idea: Helix Codex, an accountable AI operating organization. Not an autonomous agent. An organization with a constitution, named roles with bounded authority, evidence trails, and a human at every consequential boundary. Helix Prime is its operations core. Helix Education is a component of Helix Codex. It is maintained by one person, with no team and no funding. It has not been externally audited and it has not made revenue.
